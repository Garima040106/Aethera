THE FORECAST

Weather and smoke change each other. The forecast should too.

When smoke from stubble burning gets trapped near the ground by an inversion, it blocks sunlight, cools the surface and slows the wind, which traps more smoke. Aethera steps weather and pollution forward together, hour by hour, for 72 hours.

The dashboard shows inversion strength, the path of each fire plume and when it arrives, with the source and age of every number.

THE COMPANION

Health checks that never leave the phone.

Heart rate, blood oxygen, temperature, activity, sleep and falls are read and understood on the device. It learns what is normal for one person and flags heat stress, dehydration, fatigue and breathing trouble.

Only a coarse risk level and area can leave the phone, and only when the user turns sharing on. Alerts are in Hindi or English and can be read aloud.

THE CARE CIRCLE

One calm message to family, not thirty alerts.

Agents decide what to do from the forecast, her readings and whether she replies. They start with her, then her son, then an emergency contact. She can cancel before an SOS goes out.

Every decision is logged, so anyone can open "Why did I get this alert?" and see the steps.
