# Saans Saathi: Data Sources, Provenance & Honest Limitations

## 1. Environmental Data Sources & Provenance

Saans Saathi connects four core environmental data streams, providing verifiable provenance, update frequencies, and caching rules:

| Stream | Source Entity | Access Protocol | Update Cadence | Key Variables | Failover / Replay Mode |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Station Air Quality** | Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) | CAAQMS REST Portal | Hourly | PM2.5, PM10, $O_3$, $NO_2$, AQI | High-fidelity CAAQMS station replay fixture |
| **Global Air Quality** | OpenAQ Platform | OpenAQ REST API v2 | 10–30 min | PM2.5, PM10 | Cached OpenAQ station readings |
| **Active Fire Hotspots** | NASA FIRMS | MODIS / VIIRS S-NPP | 3–6 hours (Satellite pass) | Lat, Lon, Brightness Temp, Confidence | 7 verified Punjab/Haryana hotspot clusters |
| **Meso-Scale Weather** | NOAA GFS 0.25 / IMD | AWS Registry of Open Data (S3) | 6 hours | Wind Speed, Wind Dir, Temp, Humidity, PBLH | Baseline diurnal winter meteorological profile |

## 2. Distinction Between Real, Cached, and Simulated Outputs

In compliance with hackathon transparency guidelines:

1. **Observed Data:** Real CPCB station readings and NASA FIRMS satellite thermal anomalies are timestamped with elapsed time (e.g., "18 min ago", "41 min ago").
2. **Cached Data:** When network connectivity is intermittent, previously fetched payloads are served from client `localStorage` with explicit `is_cached: true` badges.
3. **Coupled Surrogate Forecast:** 72-hour forward predictions are generated by our calibrated coupled feedback solver (`SaansSaathi-CoupledSurrogate-v1.2`). They are clearly distinguished from raw observations.
4. **Demonstration Health Telemetry:** Wearable vitals (HR 96, SpO2 93%) on the Companion page are simulated to demonstrate the trigger of the "Are you ok?" check-in.
5. **Alert Workflows:** Simulated Step Functions alert ladder executions are clearly watermarked as simulations. No unauthorized emergency 112 calls or unconsented messages are transmitted.

## 3. Important System Limitations

- **Not a Medical Diagnostic Device:** Saans Saathi is a preventive lifestyle and caregiver awareness platform. It does not diagnose respiratory or cardiovascular pathologies.
- **Inversion Measurement Proxy:** Inversion strength is computed via ventilation coefficient ($V_c = \text{PBLH} \times U_{10}$). It is an honest mathematical proxy, not a direct radiosonde atmospheric sounding.
- **Plume Attribution:** Smoke plumes display forward Lagrangian advection trajectories based on forecast wind vectors. They do not claim definitive legal source attribution for specific agricultural parcels.
